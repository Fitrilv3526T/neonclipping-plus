Anton and Montserrat, (c) Google LLC, released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
Free for personal and commercial use. Full licence text:
https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/

The font files themselves are NOT stored in this repository: run
`python fonts.py --download` (or the Colab setup) to fetch them once from the
google/fonts repository into this folder. The app works fine without them
(it falls back to the fonts installed on the machine).
